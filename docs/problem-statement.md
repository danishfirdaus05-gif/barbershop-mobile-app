# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?

Customers who get haircuts at independent barbershops, and the barbers who serve them.

## What exactly is going wrong?

Customers cannot see when their preferred barber is free, so they walk in and wait or make trips for nothing. They also struggle to describe or remember the style they want, which leads to miscommunication.

## Why does it matter?

Customers lose time and sometimes end up with a haircut they didn’t want. Barbers deal with uneven waiting queues and unhappy customers, which can cost them repeat business.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)

Customers who get haircuts at barbershops cannot tell in advance when their preferred barber is free, so they often walk in and wait or travel for nothing. They also struggle to describe or remember the hairstyle they want, which leads to miscommunication and unwanted results. This wastes time for customers and creates avoidable stress and lost business for barbers. A real experience, sometime in September, I waited around one hour at Dr Muslim Barbershop because I couldn’t tell if my barber was free.
