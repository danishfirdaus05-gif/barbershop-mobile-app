# BarberBook

A mobile app that helps customers and barbers manage haircut appointments by showing barber availability and saving hairstyle preferences.

## Problem statement

Customers who get haircuts at barbershops cannot tell in advance when their preferred barber is free, so they often walk in and wait or travel for nothing. They also struggle to describe or remember the hairstyle they want, which leads to miscommunication and unwanted results. This wastes time for customers and creates avoidable stress and lost business for barbers. A real experience, sometime in September, I waited around one hour at Dr Muslim Barbershop because I couldn’t tell if my barber was free.
